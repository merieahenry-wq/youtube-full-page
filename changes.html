It is your duty to support anyone who supports you.

In this life, one faces a lot of struggles. Some find life extremely hard to cope in. So when someone offers you help, you have to repay in kind. It is not an option, but a mandatory course of action. Thats how life is, even if you disagree, thats how life should be.

One must remain in control not to take the hurt of others to heart. Especially when that person helped you so much. What goes around will always come around in this life. Support should go around, help will go around, good will go around and repaying your debt should always go around. I have always had the belief that when you help someone, its not right for the person you helped to demand for help too. I saw this as a business transaction. I saw this as something that wasn't right. I thought this was an ideal lifestyle. I embraced it. I thought it to be right.

Until I eventually knew better, I realised that this pattern was not the right one. I realised that this pattern of thought led people think you are stingy and ungrateful. This idea made complete sense to me until I started to learn to socialise more often. From my new perspective, I saw that for there to be social harmony, you have to repay every deed that was done for you. No one really gives anything for free. People usually give so that they can build a likeness with you or something else entirely. Not to say that it's a bad thing but that's what makes the world go round.

Helping those who help you should be at the core of all of our philosophy. Even helping those who has done nothing for us is also a proper line of action.
